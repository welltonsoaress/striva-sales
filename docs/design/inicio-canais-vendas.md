# Imagem original de Início

Gerada e editada em 05/10/2026 com a ferramenta integrada de imagens. Pedido: uma composição nova para a dashboard e ícones reconhecíveis do WhatsApp e Instagram. Não reutiliza a fotografia anterior nem a imagem do concorrente.

Arquivo consumido por `app/app/inicio/_client.tsx`: `public/marketing/inicio-canais-vendas.png`. A ilustração é decorativa: não indica que os canais da organização estão conectados. O estado de conexão continua vindo dos dados reais da área de trabalho.

## Prompt de criação

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original welcome dashboard hero illustration for Striva Sales, a friendly customer service and sales CRM. Primary request: create a completely new visual, not based on a stock photo or competitor image. Scene: beautifully crafted miniature tabletop still life representing a conversation becoming an organized next step. Main subject: a sculptural matte ivory smartphone standing beside two overlapping lavender and violet folded-paper conversation bubbles, a small ivory appointment calendar with a single mint green check, and a tidy stack of warm white customer cards. Objects are physical, tactile and convincing, arranged as one coherent elegant composition, not floating UI screenshots. Style: premium editorial 3D clay-and-paper diorama, soft afternoon daylight, subtle paper grain and gentle real shadows. Palette: warm off-white stone background, rich violet accents, pale lavender, one small muted mint detail; no neon, no gradient mesh. Composition: square image, main objects centered in the central two-thirds so it crops gracefully to a vertical 4:5 panel and a horizontal mobile banner. Keep the bottom 20 percent quiet for an HTML caption overlay. Mood: calm, welcoming, practical and optimistic. Text: none. Avoid all letters, numbers, logos, watermark, readable interface, charts, money symbols, robots and human faces. Do not recreate the previous woman holding a phone or the supplied competitor artwork.
```

## Prompt de edição

```text
Edit this dashboard illustration. Keep the warm stone tabletop, ivory smartphone, calendar, stacked customer cards, soft natural daylight, tactile clay-and-paper material, violet palette and camera composition. Replace the three white dots on the larger left lavender conversation bubble with a prominent authentic WhatsApp logo: recognizable bright green round chat shape with white telephone handset inside. Replace the three dots on the right violet conversation bubble with a prominent authentic Instagram app icon: rounded square pink-purple-orange gradient with a crisp white rounded-square camera outline, circle in the center, and small white dot at top right. These two brand icons must be easily recognizable at dashboard thumbnail size, central enough to survive responsive cropping, and rendered as beautiful physical inlaid emblems with the same perspective and soft shadow as the bubbles. Do not add words, letters, numbers, slogans, other logos or watermark. Maintain the quiet bottom strip for an HTML caption. The goal is to clearly evoke WhatsApp and Instagram conversations being organized into contacts and appointments, instead of generic chat symbols.
```
