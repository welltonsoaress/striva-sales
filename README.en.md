# Striva Sales — your sales operation, always present

[Português](README.md) · English · [Español](README.es.md)

Striva Sales connects WhatsApp service, follow-up, CRM, scheduling and management reports so businesses can track opportunities and keep conversations moving.

## Current commercial offering

As confirmed by the owner on September 30, 2026, Striva Sales is a closed commercial product operated by the platform team. Customers contract the solution for their business. The team manages deployment, infrastructure and updates; customers configure and oversee their operation through the access provided.

This replaces the earlier positioning as an open-source CRM for customers to install on their own VPS. The existing infrastructure kit is an operational tool for the platform team. Pricing, billing frequency, support and package terms are defined during contracting.

## How it works

1. Configure channels, knowledge, team permissions, funnels and scheduling for the business.
2. A digital sales employee handles WhatsApp conversations using the configured knowledge and rules.
3. Conversations produce CRM records and stage changes within the agent's authorized funnels.
4. Configured follow-ups continue conversations; Radar highlights risk and missing next steps.
5. Managers review the operation in the platform and through the verified WhatsApp management assistant. People can take over with context.

The platform serves clinics, professional offices, service businesses and other WhatsApp sales operations. It includes Google Calendar integration, tasks, activity history, privacy controls, daily reports and weekly comparisons with suggested priorities. Manager requests to change the CRM require confirmation. Reports use recorded operational data; they do not imply measured clinical outcomes or accounting revenue. Results depend on configuration and operation.
